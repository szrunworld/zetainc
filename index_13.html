通用工作准则（适用于所有岗位）General working principle · all roles

所有的岗位都要做好自己岗位职责内的事情，确保公司的流程化。 Every role must complete the work within their own remit so that the company stays process-driven.

不要给其他部门制造麻烦，确保自己手里的工作都能做好，不要把自己的问题转移到下一个部门。 Do not create trouble for other departments — finish your own work properly and never pass your problems on to the next department.
